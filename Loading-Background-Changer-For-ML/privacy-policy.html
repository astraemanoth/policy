1. Introduction & Overview

Loading Changer for ML (package name: com.chuchu.LoadingChangerBG) is an educational visual simulator, tutorial companion, and creative studio designed for fans and video creators. The App enables users to simulate authentic startup loading screens, test custom MP4 animations, and explore video editing tutorials safely.

We respect your privacy and believe in minimal data collection. The App does not require you to register an account, sign in, or provide personal contact details.

Key Commitment: The App operates entirely within its own sandboxed environment. We do not transmit your personal media, files, or identity to any remote server operated by us.

2. Information We Collect

A. Information You Provide to Us Directly

We do NOT require or collect any of the following directly:

  • No user accounts, usernames, or passwords.
  • No contact information (email addresses, phone numbers, postal addresses).
  • No financial, billing, or payment information.
  • No biometric or location/GPS data.

B. Information Collected Automatically by Third Parties

When you use the App, third-party advertising partners—specifically Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK)—may automatically collect certain non-personally identifiable technical information in order to serve ads and detect fraudulent activity:

  • Device Identifiers: Android Advertising ID (AAID), unique device identifiers.
  • Network & Connection: IP address, general coarse location (country/city level derived from IP), mobile network carrier.
  • Device Characteristics: Device model, operating system version, screen resolution, language settings.
  • Diagnostic & Performance Data: Ad interaction data, impression timestamps, crash logs, and load latency.

3. How We Use Information

Any technical data processed is utilized exclusively for the following legitimate purposes:

  • App Functionality: Maintaining local preferences (e.g., sound effects, simulator display settings) stored locally on your device via shared_preferences.
  • Advertising Delivery: Displaying non-intrusive App Open ads and Interstitial ads via Google AdMob to support the continued development and maintenance of the free application.
  • Quality & Stability: Monitoring crash statistics and technical performance to deliver bug fixes and performance updates.
  • Fraud Prevention & Security: Preventing click fraud, unauthorized bot traffic, and abuse of ad networks.

4. Device Permissions & Purpose

In strict compliance with Android 15 (API level 35) requirements and Google Play Developer Program policies, our app requests only the absolute minimum set of standard, non-sensitive permissions:

Permission Type Purpose / Justification
android.permission.INTERNET Normal Required to load and display advertisements via Google Mobile Ads SDK and fetch remote web fonts (Google Fonts).
android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Normal Enables the App to verify whether an active internet connection exists before requesting ad units, saving device battery and data.
Zero Sensitive Permissions: The App does NOT request sensitive or dangerous permissions such as MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, CAMERA, RECORD_AUDIO, or ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION.

5. Local Media & Custom Studio Privacy

The App features a Custom Studio where users may select an MP4 video or image file from their device to test how it looks within the simulated loading screen engine.

  • System File Picker: File selection is handled through the standard Android system photo/file picker (file_picker). The App only receives temporary read access to the specific file you voluntarily choose.
  • Local Processing Only: All video rendering, frame decoding, and playback occur 100% locally on your device hardware.
  • No Cloud Transmission: We never upload, transmit, duplicate, or inspect your selected media files. Your personal photos and videos never leave your phone.
  • Safe Media Export: When exporting tutorial presets or wallpapers, files are saved directly into your device's standard public Downloads/ directory for your personal use.

7. Data Retention & Deletion

Since Loading Changer for ML does not maintain user accounts or cloud databases, we do not store any personal data on our servers:

  • App Settings & Cache: Settings (such as sound effects and simulator preferences) are stored in local key-value pairs (SharedPreferences) on your device. You can immediately delete this data at any time by clearing the App's storage in Android Settings > Apps > Loading Changer for ML > Clear Storage / Clear Cache, or by uninstalling the application.
  • Ad Network Data: Data collected by Google AdMob is retained according to Google's standard data retention policies (typically anonymized after 9 to 18 months).
