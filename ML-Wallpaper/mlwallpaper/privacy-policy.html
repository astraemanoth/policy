1. Introduction & Overview

ML Wallpaper (package identifier: chuchu.mobile.loadwallpaper) is an aesthetic personalization and wallpaper companion application created for fans. The App provides high-definition wallpapers, lock screen art, and hero previews for personal non-commercial mobile device personalization.

Privacy By Design: You can browse, preview, and set wallpapers freely without ever creating an account or providing personal details.

2. Information We Collect

A. Information You Provide

We do NOT collect, ask for, or store any personal data:

  • No user accounts, usernames, or passwords.
  • No email addresses, telephone numbers, or physical addresses.
  • No credit card, banking, or payment data.
  • No private photos, microphone recordings, or GPS location.

B. Information Processed Automatically

To ensure app stability and deliver remote wallpaper catalogs, standard non-identifying technical data may be exchanged during normal HTTP/HTTPS requests:

  • Device Technical Information: Device model, OS version, display dimensions/density, and language setting (used to deliver appropriately sized wallpapers).
  • Network Information: IP address (utilized solely to route requested image files and discarded thereafter).

3. How Information Is Used

Any technical data processed is used strictly to:

  • Display and download high-resolution wallpapers smoothly.
  • Format wallpapers to your screen aspect ratio.
  • Maintain basic app settings (such as favorite wallpapers or dark mode) stored locally on your device.
  • Diagnose technical bugs and crashes to ensure consistent performance.

4. Device Permissions & Scoped Storage

ML Wallpaper adheres strictly to modern Android Scoped Storage standards and Google Play's Permissions and APIs That Access Sensitive Information policies.

Permission Type Purpose
android.permission.INTERNET Normal Required to load wallpaper catalogs and image previews from content delivery networks.
android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Normal Allows the App to detect active Wi-Fi or cellular connections before attempting image downloads.
android.permission.SET_WALLPAPER Normal Enables the user to directly apply selected images as the home screen or lock screen wallpaper.
Zero Sensitive Permissions: The App does NOT request MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, READ_CONTACTS, or CAMERA.

5. Wallpaper Downloads & Device Storage

When you choose to save a wallpaper to your phone:

  • The image is saved to the standard public Pictures/Wallpapers or Downloads directory on your device.
  • The App does not inspect, read, or catalog any of your personal photos, files, or documents.
  • Saved images belong entirely to you and can be managed or deleted through your gallery app at any time.

6. Third-Party Services & Advertising

The App may utilize trusted third-party SDKs (such as Google Mobile Ads / Google Play Services) to support free app operations:

  • Google Play Services: Used to maintain core framework compatibility and app distribution.
  • Google AdMob (if active): May use anonymous Advertising IDs (AAID) to display ads. Users can manage or reset their advertising ID at any time via Settings > Google > Ads on Android.

For details on how Google processes data, please consult Google's Privacy Policy.

7. Data Retention & Deletion

Because we do not operate remote databases or user accounts, we retain no personal data:

  • Favorite wallpapers and local preferences are stored only on your physical device.
  • You can delete all local data immediately by clearing the App cache/storage in Android Settings > Apps > ML Wallpaper > Storage > Clear Storage, or by uninstalling the app.

8. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

The App is not directed toward children under 13 years of age (or under 16 in the EEA/UK). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe any personal data has been inadvertently collected, please contact us immediately for prompt deletion.

10. User Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Under applicable data protection laws (such as GDPR in Europe and CCPA/CPRA in California), you have rights regarding your data:
